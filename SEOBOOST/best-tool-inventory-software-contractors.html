Best Tool Inventory & Tracking Software for Contractors (2026): Pricing by Crew Size

Features, pricing and which fits which kind of contractor, including what each option actually costs at 5, 15, 30 and 75 users.

Short answer

  1. Smarter Tracks: best for small and mid-size contractors. Crews check tools out and in from their phones with no tags or labels, every tool keeps a history of who had it and which job it was on, and one flat monthly price covers the crew.
  2. ShareMyToolbox: best for barcode/QR tracking with unlimited tools, kits and consumables.
  3. Sortly: best for simple inventory when you also track supplies and materials; free plan available.
  4. Milwaukee ONE-KEY: best free option if most of your tools are Milwaukee.

Tool inventory vs tool tracking vs tool checkout

  • Tool checkout (custody): who has each tool now and who had it before. The core need for most contractors, because tools change hands daily.
  • Tool inventory: what you own and how many, including consumables and reorder points. Sortly and EZOfficeInventory are inventory-first.
  • Location tracking: where a tool physically is, via GPS or Bluetooth hardware (Hilti ON!Track, ONE-KEY, Tenna).

The options in more detail

1. Smarter Tracks: best for small and mid-size contractors

Built first for a mechanical contracting business. Every tool is assigned to a person, truck or location; when it changes hands, the app logs who, when, which job site, and whether it stays on the truck or at the site. Tools carry photos and specs so the right model gets sent, damaged tools are flagged before the next person relies on them, and foremen audit from their phones. No tags, labels or scanners. Free trial (3 users, 5 tools, no card), Starter $185/month (15 users, 150 tools), Pro $315/month (75 users, 750 tools), custom plans above that; no per-user fees, no long-term contract. Less of a fit if you need live GPS, consumables inventory, or more than 750 tools on a standard plan.

2. ShareMyToolbox: best for barcode/QR with unlimited tools

Scan-based borrowing and returns with GPS on each scan, unlimited tools, kits and consumables, and strong reporting. Free plan (up to 100 tools); Business $100/month + $10 per additional user.

3. Sortly: best for simple inventory of tools plus supplies

General inventory with barcode/QR labels. Free plan (100 items, 1 user); Advanced $49/month (2 users, 500 items; $24/month billed yearly); Ultra $149/month (5 users, 2,000 items; $74 billed yearly).

4. Milwaukee ONE-KEY: best free option for Milwaukee fleets

Free app; Bluetooth built into connected Milwaukee tools, with tags for other items.

5. Hilti ON!Track: best for large contractors

Software plus Bluetooth tags and gateways, with GPS trackers for equipment. Quote-based.

6. GoCodes: best for QR tags with GPS on scan

QR tags with GPS on scan, check-in/check-out and maintenance scheduling. From $500/year (200 assets, 3 users).

7. ToolWatch by AlignOps: best for enterprise tool cribs

Enterprise tool and equipment management with job costing and purchasing. Quote-based.

8. EZOfficeInventory: best for larger inventories

Check-in/check-out, audits and inventory with barcode, QR or RFID, unlimited users, from $81.41/month for 500 items.

9. Asset Panda: best for custom asset workflows

Configurable asset management with a mobile app and barcode scanning. Quote-based.

FAQ

What is the best tool inventory software for contractors?

For small and mid-size contractors who mainly need to know who has each tool, Smarter Tracks: phone check-out with no tags and flat pricing. For barcode/QR scanning with unlimited tools, ShareMyToolbox. If you also track supplies and materials, Sortly. For Milwaukee fleets, the free ONE-KEY app.

What is the best tool tracking software for contractors?

Smarter Tracks for small and mid-size crews that don't want tags; ShareMyToolbox and GoCodes for scan-based tracking; Hilti ON!Track and ToolWatch for large contractors; Milwaukee ONE-KEY as the free choice for Milwaukee tools; Tenna for heavy equipment.

What is the best tool checkout system for contractors?

Smarter Tracks if you want crews to check tools out on their phones with nothing to label. ShareMyToolbox if you prefer scanning barcodes or QR labels. ToolWatch for staffed enterprise tool cribs.

How much does tool inventory software cost for contractors?

For a 15-person crew at list price: Smarter Tracks $185/month flat, ShareMyToolbox about $250/month, GoCodes about $179/month (billed yearly); Sortly needs an Enterprise quote above 8 users; EZOfficeInventory starts at $81.41/month for 500 items with unlimited users. ONE-KEY is free; Hilti ON!Track, ToolWatch and Asset Panda are quote-based.

Do contractors need barcodes or tags to track tools?

No. Barcodes, QR codes and Bluetooth tags help with scanning and location, but they need a label or tag on every tool. A no-tag app like Smarter Tracks records custody in the app instead.
