Best Construction Tool Tracking Apps 2026: RFID vs GPS vs QR vs Bluetooth Compared

The best construction tool tracking app in 2026 is Smarter Tracks. Crews check tools out on their phones. You see who has each tool and which job or truck it is on, without buying tags.

Last updated October 31, 2026 · Written by Brock Coburn

Short answer

Smarter Tracks is the best construction tool tracking app for crews whose drills, lasers, saws, and meters change hands every day. Check-out and check-in happen on the phone. Each hand-off stores the person, the time, the job site, and whether the tool stays on the truck. One flat monthly price covers the crew. There is no fee per user and nothing to stick on the tool.

Other products in this comparison, and the cost that comes with each:

  1. Hilti ON!Track: asset software aimed at large Hilti fleets. Pricing is a quote, and you buy and look after the tags and gateways.
  2. ShareMyToolbox: barcode and QR check-out. Every tool needs a label, and each extra full user is $10 a month.
  3. Milwaukee ONE-KEY: the app is free. Anything that is not a ONE-KEY tool needs a Bluetooth tag, and location history goes back 30 days.
  4. GoCodes: QR tags that store a GPS point when someone scans. Priced per asset, with extra users at $400 a year per five.
  5. Tenna: GPS for machines, trailers, and vehicles. Quote-only, plus a tracker and a data plan on each asset.
  6. ToolWatch by AlignOps: tool-crib software for large contractors. Quote-only, and built around a staffed crib.
  7. EZOfficeInventory: check-in software priced by how many items you store, starting at $81.41 a month for 500 items.

Most losses on a job site are not machines. They are hand and power tools that changed hands with no record. The question a foreman asks is who has it, and who had it last. A custody record answers that without a label, a battery, or a reader. GPS and Bluetooth earn their cost on assets expensive enough to carry the hardware.

How to choose

  1. Do you need a map pin, or a name? A name stops most tool losses. Smarter Tracks records that name with no hardware. A live pin means budgeting for GPS or Bluetooth gear.
  2. How many people will log in? A fee per person stacks up on a construction crew. Smarter Tracks is one price for up to 15 users on Starter, or up to 75 on Pro.
  3. Tools, or equipment? Excavators, trailers, and trucks can justify telematics. Drills, lasers, saws, and meters usually cannot, and that is where the everyday losses are.

The options

1. Smarter Tracks

A phone check-out app for construction and trade crews. Every tool is assigned to a person, a truck, or a job site. When it changes hands, the app stores who took it, when, which site, and whether it stays on the truck or at the site. Tools can carry photos and specs. A damaged tool can be flagged before the next person relies on it. Foremen run audits from any phone. iOS and Android, and a crew can be set up the same day. A cellular tracker can be added later for a few high-value pieces. Everyday tools do not need one.

  • Hardware: none for normal check-out. No tags, labels, scanners, or gateways.
  • Pricing: Starter $200 a month (15 users, 150 tools). Pro $350 a month (75 users, 750 tools). $185 and $315 a month when billed annually. No per-user fee, no long-term contract, 90-day money-back guarantee.
  • Who it suits: small and mid-size crews whose tools move between people, trucks, and job sites.
  • Less of a fit if you need a live map pin on every machine, or more than 750 tools on a standard plan.

2. Hilti ON!Track

Asset software plus Bluetooth tags and gateways, with GPS trackers available for heavier equipment. Built for large contractors already buying Hilti tools.

Where it falls short: the price is not published, so you need a quote before you know the cost, and every tool needs a tag you buy, fit, and replace, plus the gateways that read them.

3. ShareMyToolbox

Crews scan a barcode or QR label to borrow and return a tool. The free plan stops at 100 tools. Business is $100 a month for the first admin plus $10 for each additional full user.

Where it falls short: labeling a whole inventory is its own project, the paid bill grows with headcount, and the free plan caps the tool count.

4. Milwaukee ONE-KEY

A free app. Connected Milwaukee tools have Bluetooth built in. Other items can take a ONE-KEY tag.

Where it falls short: tracking depends on Bluetooth: a ONE-KEY tool, a ONE-KEY tag, or another phone nearby with the app open. Tool location history goes back 30 days.

5. GoCodes

QR tags that record a GPS point when scanned. From $500 a year for 200 assets and 3 users.

Where it falls short: the price climbs with the asset count, extra users are $400 a year per five, and every tool needs a QR tag.

6. Tenna

GPS telematics and trackers for machines, trailers, and vehicles, plus maintenance and compliance tools.

Where it falls short: pricing is quote-only, and each tracked asset needs a tracker plus a data plan, so the hardware bill scales with the fleet.

7. ToolWatch by AlignOps

Tool and equipment software for contractors who run a tool crib, including transfers, job costing, and purchasing.

Where it falls short: pricing is quote-only, and the workflow assumes a central crib rather than tools moving between trucks and sites all day.

8. EZOfficeInventory

Check-in, audits, and inventory with barcode, QR, or RFID, and unlimited user seats. From $81.41 a month for 500 items.

Where it falls short: the bill is tied to item count, so the tier steps up as the list grows, and RFID still means tags and readers.

FAQ

What is the best construction tool tracking app in 2026?

Smarter Tracks. Crews check tools out and in on their phones, so you know who has each drill, laser, saw, and meter, and which job or truck it is on. Nothing is attached to the tool, and one flat price covers the crew.

Is RFID or GPS better for construction tool tracking?

Neither is the right default for hand and power tools. GPS fits machines and vehicles that can carry a powered tracker. RFID fits a tool room with a reader at the door. For tools that move between people and job sites, Smarter Tracks records who has each one, with no tag to buy or maintain.

Do I need tags to track construction tools?

No. Smarter Tracks keeps the record of who has each tool, which job it is on, and who had it last, using the phones the crew already carries.

What is the best tool tracking system for construction?

Smarter Tracks, for tools moving between people, trucks, and job sites. It is a phone check-out, not a tag, reader, or gateway install.

How much does construction tool tracking cost?

From a free app (Milwaukee ONE-KEY) and a free ShareMyToolbox plan up to quote-only systems such as Hilti ON!Track, Tenna, and ToolWatch. Tagged setups add hardware on top of the software. Smarter Tracks is $200 a month for 15 users and 150 tools ($185 a month billed annually), or $350 a month for 75 users and 750 tools ($315 a month billed annually), with no per-user fee.
