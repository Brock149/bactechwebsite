Best Tool Inventory Software for Contractors (2026): Pricing by Crew Size

The best tool inventory software for contractors is Smarter Tracks. Crews check tools out on their phones, and the monthly price stays flat at 5, 15, 30, and 75 users.

Last updated October 31, 2026 · Written by Brock Coburn

Short answer

Smarter Tracks is the best tool inventory software for small and mid-size contractors who need to know who has each tool. Check-out happens on the phone. There is nothing to label or scan. One price covers the whole crew, whether that crew is 5 people or 75.

Other systems in this comparison, and the cost that comes with each:

  1. ShareMyToolbox: barcode and QR scanning. A label on every tool, and $10 a month for each extra full user.
  2. Sortly: a general inventory app for tools plus supplies. Plans cap both items and user seats.
  3. Milwaukee ONE-KEY: a free app. Tracking anything that is not a ONE-KEY tool needs a Bluetooth tag.
  4. Hilti ON!Track: tags and gateways for large contractors. The price is a quote.
  5. GoCodes: QR tags with a GPS point on each scan. Priced per asset, with extra users at $400 a year per five.
  6. ToolWatch by AlignOps: enterprise tool cribs. Quote-only.
  7. EZOfficeInventory: inventory and check-in priced by item count, from $81.41 a month for 500 items.
  8. Asset Panda: configurable asset software. Quote-only, and set up around your own workflows.

What a contractor checkout should do

  1. Log every hand-off with the person, the time, and the job or truck it went to.
  2. Show who had it last when a tool is missing or comes back broken.
  3. Work on a phone at the job site, not only at the shop.
  4. Make damage obvious before a broken tool goes back out.
  5. Make an audit quick: what each person or truck should have, versus what is actually there.

That is the job Smarter Tracks is built for. A count of consumables, or a live pin on an excavator, is a different job.

Checkout, inventory, and location

  • Checkout (custody): who has the tool now, and who had it before. This is the daily problem for most contractors. Smarter Tracks covers it with no hardware.
  • Inventory counts: how many you own, including supplies and reorder points. Sortly and EZOfficeInventory are built around counts, and their plans are capped or priced by item.
  • A physical location: a GPS or Bluetooth reading. Hilti ON!Track and ONE-KEY do this, and you buy and maintain the tags. Tenna does it for machines and vehicles, on a quote, with a tracker per asset.

The options

1. Smarter Tracks

Built first inside a mechanical contracting business. Every tool sits with a person, a truck, or a location. When it moves, the app stores who took it, when, which job site, and whether it stays on the truck or at the site. Photos and specs travel with the tool so the right model gets sent. Damaged tools are flagged before the next person depends on them. Foremen audit from their phones. No tags, labels, or scanners. A cellular tracker can be added later for a few high-value pieces.

  • Hardware: none for normal check-out.
  • Pricing: Starter $200 a month (15 users, 150 tools). Pro $350 a month (75 users, 750 tools). $185 and $315 a month when billed annually. No per-user fee, no long-term contract, 90-day money-back guarantee.
  • Who it suits: contractors whose tools move between people, trucks, and jobs, and who want a name on every tool without labeling the whole list.
  • Less of a fit if you mainly track consumables, need a live pin on every machine, or have more than 750 tools on a standard plan.

2. ShareMyToolbox

Scan a barcode or QR label to borrow and return a tool, with a GPS point stored on the scan. Unlimited tools on the paid plan, plus kits and consumables.

Where it falls short: a label on every tool, $10 a month for each extra full user past the first admin, and a free plan that stops at 100 tools.

3. Sortly

A general inventory app with barcode and QR labels, including a free plan. Paid plans start at $49 a month, or $24 a month billed yearly, for a small seat count.

Where it falls short: plans cap items and seats (free: 100 items and 1 user; Advanced: 500 items and 2 users). A full crew needs a higher tier, and Enterprise is a quote from 12 users.

4. Milwaukee ONE-KEY

A free app. Connected Milwaukee tools have Bluetooth built in. Other items can take a tag.

Where it falls short: tracking runs on Bluetooth (a ONE-KEY tool, a ONE-KEY tag, or a nearby phone with the app), and location history goes back 30 days.

5. Hilti ON!Track

Software plus Bluetooth tags and gateways, with GPS trackers for equipment. Aimed at large contractors.

Where it falls short: the price is not published, and you buy and maintain a tag on every tool plus the gateways that read them.

6. GoCodes

QR tags that store a GPS point when scanned, with check-in and maintenance scheduling. From $500 a year for 200 assets and 3 users.

Where it falls short: priced per asset, extra users at $400 a year per five, and a QR tag on every tool.

7. ToolWatch by AlignOps

Enterprise tool and equipment software with job costing and purchasing, for contractors who run a crib.

Where it falls short: quote-only pricing, and the product is built around a central crib rather than tools moving between trucks and jobs.

8. EZOfficeInventory

Check-in, audits, and inventory with barcode, QR, or RFID, and unlimited users. From $81.41 a month for 500 items.

Where it falls short: the price is tied to item count, and the tier steps up as the asset list grows.

9. Asset Panda

Configurable asset software with a mobile app and barcode scanning.

Where it falls short: quote-only pricing, and the system is configured to your workflows rather than ready to use on day one.

How much does tool inventory software cost for contractors?

Do contractors need barcodes or tags to track tools?

No. Smarter Tracks keeps the custody record in the app. Labels and Bluetooth tags are extra hardware, not a requirement for knowing who has a tool.
